I love my wing.  After I launched my phone had fallen from the flight deck.  As I was one handed getting it back in, I took a big collapse that left my wing perhaps 30% collapsed.

* I was very happy with my instinct to tuck and lean this time!
* I had to use a lot of stabilio a few times to clear it.
* The wing did not have a tendency to want to do a huge dive, which is nice!

The rest of the flight was nice.  Top of lift was around 2,350m.  Not enough to do a transition.  Was flying with lots of other sail planes.

Thomas went a bit more away from the hill and landed, so, I did a great spiral dive (- ~9m/s) and went and joined him to finish on a high.

Great way of finishing my Switzerland trip :)
