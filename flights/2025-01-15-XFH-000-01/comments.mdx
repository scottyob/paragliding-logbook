Probably one of the best flights of my life. We were waiting for a cycle to launch into. It was our “test day” to see how we all went. 
- Jason Seto and I were lagging a little to launch and had to wait for a right cycle and the wind to be working when we launched. 
- it was working as soon as I launched and stayed high. 
- at launch I saw Jason very deep and back, the windsocks at launch looked catabolic and I took a 40% collapse that came right out around there, so decided to play out more in front.  
- went exploring north, didn’t find anything, came back to launch and joined up with Dave Oddy and Muuo. 
- proceeded to have an amazing flight “cloud hopping”. I can see why this is so special. 
- we came down to land once it started raining to our north. Winds picked up soon after landing so I think was the right conservative call.
