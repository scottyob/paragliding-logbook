This was a fun flight. 

* Forecast was to have top of lift and cloud cover around 6,000 ft.
* I reached cloud base at around 1pm at 4,000 ft
* We watched someone launch a few minutes before me, they head north to the slide, and sunk out as we were getting clouded over (down cycle)
* I launched next when the sun had come back out, did one pass of the slide, didn’t find much, so was heading over towards the horses heaven.  Didn’t have the altitude to get there, but, found something to fight for on the way that got me back.
* Watched Abhi launch in a good cycle, got and stayed high, then as I was still fighting to get altitude, he head south, down past Alum rock.
* Someone else launched and also was staying high from the get go.  Was interesting to watch their flight as they stayed on the ridge line for the most part, then sunk around the slide as I was finding fantastic climbs out front.

My flight ended as I fell out of a thermal and was heading back towards the LZ.  Was hoping to find a good climb under a darkish cloud, but, found big sink on the way.

Some key learnings:
* I think when it’s a strong, very north day, the slide can act as a sink on the northern end.
* On very north days, I seem to find the horses heaven area tends to work better, and head out front on the way to get there.
