Eventful flight!



Second flight launch was from the western one. Some highlights (why do I not have my camera on for these):



*   I did NOT get stuck in a tree
*   Tried to head over to Giferspitz, got close, sail planes scared me and I left so they could have the hole thing to themselves. At the speed they were circling me, there’s not much I can do to keep out of their way and just have to hope they’ve seen me.  I wish I had flarm out working. 
*   Went to the launch on the west side, fighting headwind. Interesting to see how high I can smell smoke
*   Started down to the next ridge, was getting high watching a sail plane struggle on the lower ridge
*   Didn’t see a general aircraft until it was right in front of me. Level with me, maybe 200m in front.  They did a wing waggle. Was it to say “I saw you” or “oh sorry; didn’t see you”
*   Can’t remember why, but I didn’t try and make it past Gsteig.  Pushing into wind without the same cloud markers. 
*   Sail planes above me back at the wispile 
*   Got so scared, thought it was a sail plane at first. There was an eagle so close to me I thought it was going to fly into my face!  I stayed with it for maybe a minute or two. It was close enough to count features!  Maybe a common buzzard, or booted eagle??
*   Afternoon was wearing on. Just in a lazy thermal watching two fighter jets above and off in the distance. I think they were playing mock fights with one another.  So cool to watch!
*   Didn’t find lift over to the western mountain launch again. As I was getting lower, a helicopter passed directly above me!  I was ready for turbulence, but it must have been just a bit above for the wake to be blown away. 
*   Very lifty and windy landing
*   As I was packing; a jet flew low right over the landing zone!  About where I was setting up my approach!  So glad I landed when I did. 



How cool!  Saw all the things!!
