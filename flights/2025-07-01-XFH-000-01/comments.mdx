First flight in Switzerland!

Take off is on the “East” launch in the morning.  Launch was super early, and there were big booming clouds.

This site’s east launch has Beta to “hang a left right away, maybe find a climb over the trees or the rocks on the way, but don’t get too low, else you won’t make it around the gondola to the LZ”

The weather was forecast to have storms from midday on.  I landed when I was a little concerned with how quickly the clouds were developing, but, ended up not storming here for a bit over 2+ hours.

Good first flight for the area, but, I need to learn to be a little less conservative maybe.  My flights should be longer when I can judge the wind better.

FlySkyHy was annoying me here, as, the airspace is far, far too verbose.
