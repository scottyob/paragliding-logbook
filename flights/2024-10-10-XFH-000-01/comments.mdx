First flight on new wing!

- Unpacking was interesting.  Got lines tangles somehow when doing my inspection.
- Bringing it up is HARD! compared to other wings I've flown.  Wingtips want to do their own thing.  Took a few attempts.
- Flying itself felt great.
- So much glide in light winds to get to Mussel rock and back to the ridge so easily.
- Got to be careful with big ears taking a while to "work out".  They do not come out on their own.  Manual says:
 - One at a time, easy gentle movements.
