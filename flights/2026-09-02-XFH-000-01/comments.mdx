This flight was supposed to be from Ed -> Mt. Hamilton!

Had planned on flying with James, and, Rai ended up joining us!

We took off from the 600’, and, got plenty of altitude.  Rai was on radio saying that he thinks a better plan may be Livermore via Mission!

Lots of sink on the way to Mission, with strong climbs. Plenty of climbs out at Mission, and, enough cumulus’s to make it feel like a mini Colombia!

Once I head out east from Mission, gosh there was a lot of sink.  

I made a bad move near the water boards land, and, ended up finding a good landing option, decided to try and follow the low foothills to the main road to make Uber easier, but, that was a mistake as it was windy there and ended up landing backwards!! Lots of learnings from this lesson. I’m going to add some personal no landing zones on my instrument too to help me be better informed when I decide to head out this way on a whim.
