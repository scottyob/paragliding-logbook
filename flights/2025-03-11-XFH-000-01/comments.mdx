Decided not to go close behind the ridge on the north in case of rotor. Scratch around and found one really nice climb up. To launch height. 

Last mistake was b-lining it back up to launch, but wasn’t much lift to be had here. Looks like the folks at mission did better. Still fun!!

Had to be careful landing. Winds were very northy, catabatic at times.
