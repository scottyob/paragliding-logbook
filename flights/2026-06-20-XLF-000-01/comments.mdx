What a wonderful day!  The day was forecast to be a bit more northy and
gustier than yesterday, so, plan after we spoke about it on launch was to
head out early.

Originally, I had planned on trading a run with Bethany, but, by the time
we were seeing people take off at 10:15 and find lift, we decided to all go
fly and worry about the car after.

I took off at 10:45, found a good climb soon after.  My goal was to at
least make it past the Vineyard.

Just past 11, I had reached my little goal.  There was much more air
traffic than I had expected in that area.  Towards 11:30, I decided the
climbs and sink was a bit too strong, and the wind was already picking up,
so it was safer to land.

I didn't have any fly buddies flying with me during this flight, but, Honda
crushed it and top landed for retrieve, and, it was nice of everyone to
join after.
