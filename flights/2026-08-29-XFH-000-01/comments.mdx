Mini Triangle!

Goal was to try and head a little upwind to Tollhouse!

Beautiful flight with Rai, but, got down a bit low over Bald, and holy crap was it turbo!  I felt like a ragdoll under a plastic bag!  

Pretty sure the wind was a bit from the north, so, perhaps there was some rotor from the mountain a little upwind of me.  

I radio’d my “Sorry” to Rai who had said “I’ll wait here for you”. I didn’t know where “Here” was. I couldn’t see him. I was struggling to find lift in a little bowl, and, took what I think was a small full frontal. I wasn’t high enough off the deck for this shit! I’m leaving!

I turned south of Bald figuring I’ll land in the valley somewhere to make retrieve a little easier.

As I was setting up on my field for landing, I found a wonderful climb to take me back up to height there.

I had the decision, to try and head back to Bald, and try and find Rai again, or, keep seeing if I can make it back home.  Not seeing Rai, I decided it was time to see if I could make it back to the LZ, and, ended up closing a little valley tour and triangle.

Sorry, Rai!  I should have picked him up, but, by the time I landed, it was time for a sunset flight with Bethany!

Think I owe this man a beer now!
