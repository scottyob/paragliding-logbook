The task for this day was very difficult. Plan was to stay together and team fly. 

Kind of fell apart as I went north; others didn’t know to. I waited back at launch for Dave and Nick to catch up. We got the southern waypoint, but heading out to our next waypoint was our undoing. We went straight for the big cloud and it wasn’t working out front there. 

Nick and I both landed around the area, Dave continued for a tad more. Jess somehow managed to cross the valley.
