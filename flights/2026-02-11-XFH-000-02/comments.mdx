What a fun interesting flight.  Today’s lessons was one of lacking patience.

1. Left the group feeling like there would be a stronger climb out front, which wasn’t the case.  This was the first example of being impatient.
2. Was down low and saw Miguel,  Jesse pointed out that the local was at my altitude and it’d be wise to follow him.  Followed him for a while and we found a weak thermal.  This only lasted a bit before I grew impatient (again), and went to try and find a stronger part of the thermal.  Of course, I couldn’t outfly the local and decided to head to the LZ with plenty of altitude.

The end of the flight was interesting.  Over the lake, there was some solid climbs, but I couldn’t get over 6500ft.  

Maaaybe I could have made it back to the ridge, or maaaaybe I could have just hung out for an hour or two longer, but, didn’t want anyone waiting around and decided that’ll do!!

Next flights, I want to exercise patience.
