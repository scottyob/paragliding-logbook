Such different days!

This morning was completely clouded over, with the weather forecast saying we could have some showers up to midday, and good after. 

Odd that launched after midday, and a steady east in the wind, so used my favorite launch. Cored the thermals a bit tighter and that was working fine. 

Down the valley, some rain started, and Virgo not far. So spiraled down and landed. 

Met a very experienced glider, Pete!  He was showing me the routes to xc to interlaken, but, that looks too committing for me (deep valleys, strong valley winds). Pete landed due to not being sure about the clouds, so, I think that also gives confirmation and good decisions were made.
