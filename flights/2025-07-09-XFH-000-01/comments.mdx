Challenging rodeo kind of a flight!

Launch and initial climbs were fine.  Got well established, and found it very (very) bumpy.  Was trying to get high with Thomas this flight.

Went over to the west to see if I could start a XC flight.  On the first ridge, I found it like a washing machine and wanted to come back.  I was also far too close to another sailplane for comfort for me.

Weather was a lot more gusty and stronger than forecast.  It was so cold.  I was wearing by big gloves on, but when I couldn’t feel my fingertips and wasn’t enjoying my flight I called it to come in to land!

Thomas went east to say hi to his sheep!  I wish I had stayed with him.
