First flight from Cove in years!

Very small flight, but lots to take away:

- Getting ready was a challenge.  I must have spent 45 minutes fiddling with my gear.  I attribute this mostly to the altitude, and perhaps tiredness of just having drove in the day prior.

- Getting the front mount reserve on took a lot of effort.  Had it looped around the riser.  I attribute this to hypoxia.

- Once I was in the air, I found some climbs over Triceratops.  Some short thermals, and made the LZ with heaps and heaps of altitude. Landing even at higher elevation on the Maestro 2 was fantastic.
