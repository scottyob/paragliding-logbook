Europe trip off to a very strong start!  

The gym we were at yesterday, we saw heaps of gliders going off a hill in the distance all day.

Looked up the launch and their site guide website, and it turns out this weekend, they had a paragliding festival!

Lots of tents setup from lots of different brands. I saw a distributer from Ozone, and just got to chat about how much I love the wing and no need to demo (he had so many sizes here you could have demo’d Jess!)

They were running busses to the gondola and half price trips up to the top for the event!

The flight was great. Tad over an hour, could have spent all day here.  got to shake out come cobwebs and good intro to the trip. Nice to land and hang out and chat!  

Interesting site with a lot of airspace to worry about!  I have one line between the different airspace coming down the mountain where I’m just going back and forward, back and forward thinking “if I’m to loose 100 more meters to be able to enter that airspace, I’m so close to these trees and gondola”

I know it’s no excuse, but there were so many more gliders higher than me, towards the LZ, so I used that as an excuse and figured I’d chat to the others on the ground. 

Today I learned, the airspace here was ~1,000 meters, OR 300 AGL. I ended up having heaps of margin and went in a lot lower than I needed!  Flyskyhy does not mention that and just starts yelling at you!

Then the icing on the take. I disassembled my glider bag, stuffed it into a locker, and we stopped off at CERN. I got to see THE NeXT workstation that Tim Berners-Lee used to develop and run the world’s first web server. I got to fan-geek out!  What a day!
