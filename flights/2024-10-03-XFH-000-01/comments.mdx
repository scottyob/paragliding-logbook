Last flight in Utah for the year!

Plan was to stay high until 1pm, when the forecast said it was going to be best before doing a Monroe->Cove transition.

Honda left very early in deep.  I decided to chill for a while.  Found some crazy sink though and big whacks that made me pretty nervous.

Ended up pushing out front to hopefully find some nicer thermals, but nothing much.  Nothing out the front of Cove to bench back up.

With more confidence, I'm sure I can start doing the transitions much deeper, but, not this trip :)
