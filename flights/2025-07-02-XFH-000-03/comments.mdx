Went back up for a second flight with Thomas.

The winds were a bit more from the West.  The afternoon western launch was so light, and, I wasn’t at all happy with the tree hazards with how light the conditions were.

We ended up checking out the east launch again, then hiked down to the northern launch due to it being over the back there.

North launch was fun!  Launching right next to / below Gondola, but, the trees were a lot, lot less intimidating!

I found a climb right off to the left, and, believe that took me back up to over the rocks.

The winds were very shifty, and it was a little rowdy, so I was unsure and tried to push out the westerly side, figuring there might be a little more lift there.

I’ve never experienced -5m/s sink without a boom to speak of, but, I think I should have hugged the mountains more on the western side today to stay up!

Such an interesting different day to yesterday!
