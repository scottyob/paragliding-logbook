This is likely the best flight of my life. I’m writing an abridged version as the log is a little late and I’m starting for forget it:

- was trying to keep up with the group, but had some people drop off. 
- Jess and Dave really showed me where the the tough climbs were over the crocodile. 
- I had “never smile at a crocodile” in my head for that entire ridge. Apparently it’s in Nick’s head too now. 
- Was going to land near where I think Jess did. Christian was nice enough to tell me to keep pushing towards the tower / low ridge. He mentioned there’s enough ok landing spots, which there was!   We lost Christian in the flats after he spiraled down to help us, but his advice kept us in the running!
- Ended up pushing out over the flats with Dave and Ladi
- Dave was the leader here and pretty much made all of the calls. I was fortunate enough to be above him for a good duration of the flight so I’d push when he did. Dave was amazing and I want to be just like him one day!
- The words we started to use was “push, climbing, searching” on radio. 
- We lost Ladi towards the eastern sides of the flat. We think he managed to overtake us. 
- Dave took a deeper route, I took a less deep one to be more conservative so we separated towards the end
- Soon after I had what I *think* was a frontal.  Loss of pressure, followed by the wing in front of me coming down to the right, ending up in a half riser twist. I  was focusing on watching the wing and risers as not to over correct the glider until I was sure what needed to happen
- The glider sorted itself out and although I was ready to chuck the reserve if need be the wing pretty much self recovered with about 60m of altitude loss.
- I pushed out over to the flats right after to find “easy thermals” and had a nice landing. 

The landing field was a lot smaller than I anticipated. As I got lower I realized it was further sub divided, but nailed it :)

My walk out was ok as planned, but there was a compound between my access road and the highway!  As I was on google earth to find another way to the highway, some men came and assisted me through the compound to the highway, and made sure to keep the dogs from approaching me. They were very nice and spoke some English. 

This ended up being the flight of the trip for me!
