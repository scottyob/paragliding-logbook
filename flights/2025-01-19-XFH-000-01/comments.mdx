Flight log January 19

We started at a new launch today. It was just around the corner from yesterday’s. We decided on this due to the bus having trouble along the roads. 

The launch was a little tight. There were lots of people around, but I was happy with it.  had a good line and lift right off the bat. I left launch area because people were starting to get into clouds all around and it was overcrowding by far for me.

I shot down to the next ridge and Jess joined me. This was the last time I was with my group. Jess left me and I didn’t feel comfortable with the altitude that I had. I ascended higher the time. I climbed up but had lost sight of Jess.

I was extremely happy with following the ridge Northwoods deep along the ridge. Jason, who I had seen earlier very low and pushing in deeper, made a radio comment about not knowing where he is and coming out of the clouds. I tried to mention that he was well above me and that we were together, but I don’t think this was received.

Once I got to cloud base, my task was telling me that I could start heading out over the flats following Jason and it should keep taking me to the next waypoint. This is where the learnings come in

* I am extremely happy with my glider. I felt I was able to start thinking about some strategic moves instead of worrying about how low I was most of the time.
* I feel I don’t have the mental capacity just yet to be looking at the next clouds while I’m thermaling
* Heading to the flats, I was heading to the closest cloud which I thought was building. There was lots of blue sky.
* That cloud ended up petering away just as I was getting there
* I had wasted a bunch of altitude and then head to the next biggest cloud closest to me. Unfortunately, I got nothing here.
* Once I had landed at roughly midday, the cloud started filling in in the valley above me, maybe 30 or 40 minutes after I had landed
* For the next flights here I would like to be more patient along the mountains and then shoot out into the valley once it fills in a little more and is working more. 

All in all an absolutely wonderful flight. I’m now dictating this on my phone as I wander around and watching new species of birds that I haven’t seen before and looking very pictures scenes. This is absolutely wonderful. What a magical place!

Got to go!  Karen just got here with the bus <3
