Now this was a fun flight!  The wind was definitely very strong from the west. Launched the lower launch and made it up pretty easily. 

Did a little valley tour to see where I can get to, and testing the headwind on the way back. I found it exciting that the swift can push into wind and I still had tons of height to spare on the way back. 

I feel I’m starting to get the hang of this site. 

## Highlights:
* passed sail plane, twice!  The closest I don’t have on video, but totally the furthest one I do!
* a small jet? Passed directly underneath me, a few hundred meters!!

Landing was strong!  Took an asymmetric as I was setting up my landing approach but cleared it before getting too low. 

It was fun, but often I was bucking the weather. 

Didn’t wear my jacket as I didn’t expect a big flight. Was shivering up there!!
