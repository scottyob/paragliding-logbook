Goal was Roldanillo to Pedechinche

The flight was great!  Got separated from the group from the get go as I chose the route that wouldn’t be deep and always give me an easy LZ. 

My route seemed to be working better as when the others pushed out from being deep, some of the group got pretty low. 

Diego, Muuo, Nick, Arpan and myself pushed out over the flats down south but I was often finding it hard to keep up. 

Arpan caught some amazing climbs, as he does, when he was low at some point and was able to catch up with the group. 

I took the less committing route towards tulua, where I thought i saw a big fluffy cloud. This ended up being my undoing as the cloud was further away than I thought. 

I spent a while fighting there, when the skies were starting to look ominous. 

I heard Dave and Christian on radio and realized they were in the area and going to head back up north to avoid the rain. I did the same; but was fighting much lower (and skirted in airspace to find a good LZ, booo!)

Christian spiraled down to join me, but then was fighting far too low. 

I thought I’d do the helpful thing by finding him a climb, but, once again the cloud I was chasing too far in the distance. 

Landed at a water park soccer field thinking there would be beer there. It was closed :(. The workers loved our entry and took videos and even knocked some mangoes down from a tree on the fields edge!

My learnings today:
- try to look more at the shadows in the main parts of the day to mark where the clouds are over ground (thanks, Arpan)
- keep the gaggle together. Don’t hunt a different cloud when we’re out on the flats. 

My following other learnings:
- landed and avoided big clouds earlier on to avoid scary or dangerous situations. A++

I’m really happy with this.
